Our 6-Step Pipeline Process

From prospect identification to qualified meetings - here's how we fill your pipeline

01

Market Research & Targeting

Week 1

We analyze your ideal customer profile and identify high-value prospects in your target market using advanced data intelligence.

Key Activities

  • Comprehensive market analysis
  • Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) refinement
  • Prospect database building
  • Competitive landscape mapping
02

Decision-Maker Identification

Our team identifies and verifies key decision-makers within target companies, ensuring we reach the right people with buying authority.

Key Activities

  • Organizational chart mapping
  • Contact verification process
  • Authority level assessment
03

Personalized Outreach

Week 2-3

We craft highly personalized, multi-channel outreach campaigns that resonate with each prospect's specific pain points and business objectives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Pipeline-as-a-Service

How does the pay-per-meeting model work?

+

You only pay for meetings that actually happen. We charge a fixed fee per qualified meeting that shows up on your calendar. If a prospect doesn't attend, you don't pay. This ensures complete alignment between our success and yours.

What qualifies as a 'qualified meeting'?

+

A qualified meeting is with a verified decision-maker at a target company who has expressed genuine interest in your solution, has confirmed budget availability, and fits your ideal customer profile. We pre-screen all prospects to ensure quality.

How quickly can we start seeing results?

+

Most clients see their first qualified meetings within 2-3 weeks. We ramp up to full capacity by week 4-6, delivering a consistent flow of 15+ qualified meetings per month, depending on your target market and package.

What information do you provide about prospects?

+

Before each meeting, you receive a detailed dossier including: company background, prospect's role and responsibilities, pain points identified during outreach, recent company news, talking points, and competitive insights. This ensures you're fully prepared for every conversation.

Do you integrate with our existing CRM?

+

Yes, we integrate natively with all major CRMs including Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and others. Meetings automatically sync to your calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook) and prospect data flows directly into your CRM system.

What if prospects don't show up to meetings?

+

You don't pay for no-shows. Our 85% average show-up rate is backed by our confirmation process, reminder sequences, and prospect quality standards. We also reschedule no-shows at no additional cost to maximize your ROI.
